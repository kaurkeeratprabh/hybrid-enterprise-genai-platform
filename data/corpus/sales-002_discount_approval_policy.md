# Discount Approval Policy

## Purpose
This document defines the internal requirements for discount approval policy.

## Policy and procedure
Sales representatives may offer discounts up to 5% within approved price lists. Discounts above 5% require regional sales manager approval; discounts above 15% require Sales Director and Finance approval.

## Responsibilities
- **Process owner:** Maintains this document, reviews exceptions, and approves updates.
- **Employees and requesters:** Follow the procedure and provide accurate information.
- **Approver:** Reviews requests within their delegated authority and records the decision.

## Exceptions
Exceptions must be documented with the business reason, risk assessment, approving role, expiry date, and compensating controls. Verbal approval alone is not sufficient for a permanent exception.

## Evidence and records
Relevant approvals, tickets, forms, and supporting records must be stored in the approved business system with access restricted according to the information classification.
